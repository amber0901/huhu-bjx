Chinese traditional instrumental background music for a kindergarten poetry reading. Sparse delicate guzheng plucks and a very soft bamboo flute, unhurried and gentle, warm evening light in a peaceful old lane, small swallows returning to ordinary homes. Pentatonic melody, spacious arrangement, very restrained dynamics, no strong percussion and no dramatic climax. Pure instrumental, no vocals, no humming, no spoken voice. The music must leave room for a clear teacher's poetry narration. A calm beginning and ending suitable for preparing a seamless short background loop.
